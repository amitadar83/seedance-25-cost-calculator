Method & live-data health

Show the working.

Every number carries a source, a mode, and a timestamp. Automated checks create fresh snapshots; validation holds surprising changes for review.

01

Common unit

Five seconds, 16:9, 480p or 720p, native audio, text or image input, and no input video. Subscription cost assumes full monthly credit utilization.

02

Separate lanes

Seedance subscription wrappers, native-model studios, and pay-as-you-go endpoints remain distinct. Each lane answers a different buying question.

03

Deal discipline

Annual billing and temporary offers live on the Deals page. The base view uses recurring monthly list prices. Exact active offers may be layered on deliberately.

04

Sentiment limits

Venue mix, collection bias, and evidence coverage appear beside each sentiment summary. Repeated themes support directional confidence, not population prevalence.
